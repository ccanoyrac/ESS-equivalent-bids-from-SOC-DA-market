Abstract— We formulate the problem of dynamic, real-time
optimal power dispatch for electric power systems consisting
of conventional power generators, intermittent generators from
renewable sources, energy storage systems and price-inelastic
loads. The generation company managing the power system
can place bids on the real-time energy market (the so-called
regulating market) in order to balance its loads and/or to make
profit. Prices, demands and intermittent power injections are
considered to be stochastic processes and the goal is to compute
power injections for the conventional power generators, charge
and discharge levels for the storage units and exchanged power
with the rest of the grid that minimize operating and trading
costs. We propose a scenario-based stochastic model predictive
control algorithm to solve the real-time market-based optimal
power dispatch problem
